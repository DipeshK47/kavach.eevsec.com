2. What this policy covers

This policy covers this public KAVACH product website, including its demo request form, pages, assets, and privacy choices. KAVACH training deployments operate separately inside customer environments; this policy does not govern a customer’s offline appliance or training records.

3. Information we collect

  • Contact information: name, work email, organisation, organisation type, and the message you submit through the demo request form.
  • Security and delivery information: IP address, browser/device information, requested URL, timestamps, referrer, response status, and other technical information that hosting, form-delivery, or security providers may record in their logs.
  • Consent and preference information: your cookie choice in the first-party eevsec_consent cookie, and your light/dark theme preference in browser local storage only when you allow Preferences.
  • Anti-abuse information: hCaptcha may process device, interaction, and network signals when the form’s human-verification widget is loaded or used.

Please do not submit passwords, operational details, personnel data, security records, or other sensitive information through this public form.

4. Why we use information

  • To respond to demo requests and communicate about the requested service.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve this website.
  • To prevent spam, automated abuse, and fraudulent form submissions.
  • To remember your privacy and display preferences where you have allowed them.
  • To comply with legal obligations, establish or defend legal claims, and protect our rights.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on steps taken at your request before a contract, our legitimate interests in responding and protecting the site, legal obligations, and consent for optional Preferences storage. You can withdraw optional consent at any time through .

5. Service providers

We use limited providers to deliver this site and handle the demo request form:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, content delivery, DNS, security, and technical logging for the site. Cloudflare may use strictly necessary security cookies or similar technologies where its protection features require them. See Cloudflare’s cookie documentation and privacy policy.
  • Web3Forms: receives the form payload and forwards it to our designated email inbox. See Web3Forms’ privacy information.
  • hCaptcha: provides human verification and spam prevention for the form. See hCaptcha’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Google Fonts: supplies the typefaces used by the design. The browser may send a font request and associated technical information to Google. See Google Fonts’ privacy FAQ.

We do not use this site for advertising, behavioral profiling, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or other analytics/advertising cookies as of the date above. If that changes, we will update this policy and the consent choices before enabling those technologies where required.

To exercise a right, email dpo@eevsec.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding and will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Residents of California and other US states may have additional rights, including rights relating to sale, sharing, targeted advertising, and sensitive personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising on this site.

We use HTTPS, access controls, provider security controls, and reasonable operational safeguards. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. This site is intended for business users and is not directed to children under the age of digital consent in their jurisdiction.

We may update this policy when our services, providers, or legal obligations change. The updated effective date will appear at the top of this page.
