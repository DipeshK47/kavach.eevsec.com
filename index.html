WHAT IS KAVACH?

Cyber awareness, built for closed networks.

KAVACH is an offline training platform for organisations that cannot risk a breach. People learn through realistic decisions; administrators see readiness without sending data outside the network.

01

Offline by design

Runs in airgapped environments. Training data stays inside.

02

People first

Practice what to open, trust, reply to, and share.

03

Self-contained

Delivered as a local appliance for secure deployment.
